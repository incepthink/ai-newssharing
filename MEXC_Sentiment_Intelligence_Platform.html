Real-Time Social Media & Campaign Sentiment Intelligence Platform
Enterprise Architecture & Implementation Blueprint | Client Solution for MEXC
Problem
Context & Operational Challenge

MEXC required continuous 24/7 visibility into brand perception, user sentiment, and marketing campaign performance across hyper-active and fragmented cryptocurrency communities (X/Twitter, Telegram, Discord, and Reddit). The high velocity of crypto chatter—dominated by trader slang, bot-driven spam campaigns, rapid market volatility swings, and coordinated FUD (Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt: orchestrated panic rumors or smear campaigns)—rendered traditional social listening tools ineffective at distinguishing actionable market signals from noise or measuring true campaign resonance.

Solution

Architected and deployed an autonomous, end-to-end social intelligence and sentiment analysis platform designed specifically for the dynamics of crypto social ecosystems:

  • Multi-Channel Streaming Ingestion & Noise Scrubbing: Built high-throughput streaming pipelines ingesting posts, replies, and channel messages across X, Telegram, Discord, and Reddit. Deployed deterministic filters and fast duplicate-pruning (Redis Bloom filters) to eliminate bot spam, fraudulent airdrop giveaways, and cashtag hijacking (spammers attaching the $MX ticker symbol to unrelated promotional scams or fake giveaways to hijack community search feeds).
  • Semantic Topic Clustering (Vector Similarity Search): Applied AI text embeddings (text-embedding-3-large @ 1024-dim) paired with high-speed vector search (pgvector) to automatically group high-volume community chatter into distinct discussion topics in real time (e.g., app performance, withdrawal speeds, listing inquiries), without requiring manually preconfigured keyword lists.
  • Domain-Specific AI Sentiment & Campaign Engine: Deployed a self-hosted AI model (Qwen-2.5 on a private cluster) with strict output data validation. The engine decodes crypto-native trader slang (such as "rekt" [steep trading losses], "liquidated" [margin call], "dev rugged" [scam token exit], and "HODL" [holding long-term]) to classify multi-class sentiment (Bullish, Bearish, Neutral, Panic/Crisis). Crucially, it separates individual trader leverage losses from legitimate platform operational issues and attributes conversation spikes to marketing events.
  • Influencer & KOL (Key Opinion Leader) Attribution: Computed reach, account authenticity, and engagement ratios to distinguish genuine Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) from retail noise, triggering automated alerts when sudden negative sentiment anomalies or coordinated smear campaigns emerge.
  • Executive Command Center Dashboard: Delivered an interactive Next.js real-time analytics portal displaying live community mood curves, campaign engagement ROI across token launches (such as #Kickstarter and #Launchpad), sentiment share of voice against competitor exchanges, and interactive topic heatmaps.
